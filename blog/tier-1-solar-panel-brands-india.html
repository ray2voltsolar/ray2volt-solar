Tier-1 Solar Panel Brands in India (2026): Waaree, Adani, Tata & Premier

A Tier-1 badge gives you only part of the picture. Compare the actual panel model, its ALMM and DCR status, hot-weather output and warranty before approving a quotation.

Points to remember

  • BloombergNEF Tier-1 is a bankability measure used by lenders. It does not certify an individual panel's materials or manufacturing quality.
  • After the June 2026 ALMM List-II shift, check cell origin as well as module listing. DCR modules have an indicative premium of ₹9 to ₹12 per Wp.
  • N-type TOPCon can have a temperature coefficient of -0.30% per degree C and a year-30 warranted output above 87.4%. Check the actual model datasheet.
  • Waaree, Adani, Tata Power Solar and Premier Energies offer different models. Compare model construction, compliance records, serial numbers and warranty support.

A quotation that says only 'Tier-1 panels' leaves out the information you need. Ask for the brand, model, cell type and product warranty before approving it. A homeowner and a factory owner both need to know what will arrive on the roof and how to make a claim later. Put the model number in the purchase order so a substitute cannot be delivered without your consent. Keep the invoice and datasheet with the commissioning records, because a warranty claim may happen many years after the installer has finished work.

Indian module capacity has expanded beyond 60 GW, supported in part by the Production Linked Incentive scheme. Domestic factories now produce N-type TOPCon bifacial modules with efficiencies above 22.5%. Those figures describe industry capacity and selected products; they do not establish the performance of every panel a contractor might offer.

This comparison covers Waaree Energies, Adani Solar, Tata Power Solar and Premier Energies. Vikram Solar and Goldi Solar are further options to ask about. We focus on cell design, heat loss, DCR documents and support for claims. For background, see our guide to panel types and DCR and ALMM guide.

What Tier-1 means for a buyer

BloombergNEF sets Tier-1 as a financing test, not an engineering inspection. To qualify, a maker must supply its own manufactured and branded modules to at least six different commercial projects above 1.5 MW. Six different commercial banks must have financed those projects without recourse during the previous two years.

For a lender, that track record shows that banks have accepted the maker on several projects. It suggests bankability, but cannot promise that the company will never fail. Nor does it tell you how a particular module was assembled or tested.

A single maker can sell a lower-cost panel with a 30 mm aluminium frame and basic encapsulant alongside a dual-glass bifacial module using a 35 mm anodised frame and a premium backsheet. Both carry the same brand. Read the bill of materials and ask about cell sorting and electroluminescence testing before comparing them. Frame thickness matters on an exposed shed, while encapsulation and sealing matter wherever moisture can reach the laminate. Ask for the front and rear load ratings, connector type and installation manual too. A well-made module can still be damaged by unsuitable clamps or poor cable routing.

Use Tier-1 for a bankability check and ALMM for the applicable listing. Ask the installer for the exact model and factory flash-test report. The cell design and installation work determine much of the result on your roof.

Check both ALMM lists

MNRE's Approved List of Models and Manufacturers controls approved models for covered Indian projects. It serves a different purpose from BloombergNEF's list. Before paying, confirm which entries and any exemptions apply to your subsidy, net-metering or open-access project.

List-I identifies approved modules

List-I was reapplied from 1 April 2024. It covers modules for government-supported projects, covered net-metered roofs and some open-access installations. More than 50 GW of Indian module capacity was reported as listed in 2026. Your quotation still needs the exact listed model, not merely a listed maker.

List-II identifies approved cells

Indian module assemblers have often laminated imported cells into locally made modules. Since the June 2026 List-II shift, cell origin matters in covered projects. DCR panels combine Indian-made cells with Indian module assembly. PM Surya Ghar homes need DCR modules; check the specific compliance path or verified exemption for a commercial project.

Indian cell supply remains tighter than module assembly. Allow an indicative DCR premium of ₹9 to ₹12 per Wp, about ₹9,000 to ₹12,000 per kWp, against similar non-DCR panels. Ray2Volt prepares new commercial and industrial quotations with DCR panels unless a verified exemption applies. Confirm pricing and listing again at the site audit. Scheme rules and figures can change, so check eligibility for your own project. Ray2Volt does not give tax or investment advice.

Compare the four manufacturers

These profiles help frame a quotation comparison. Product ranges and capacities change, so request the current datasheet for each offered model.

Waaree Energies

Waaree has reported over 13 GW of module capacity at facilities including Surat, Chikhli and Noida. Its Aditya line uses Mono PERC, while Elite includes N-type TOPCon bifacial panels up to 590 Wp. Ask which series and rating your contractor has priced, since the Waaree name alone does not identify the technology.

Waaree's large utility and rooftop distribution network can help with replacement availability in South India. Some TOPCon models specify -0.30% per degree C as the power temperature coefficient and 87.4% as the year-30 warranted output. Check the exact datasheet and retain the delivered serial numbers.

Adani Solar at Mundra

At Mundra in Gujarat, Adani Solar operates across ingot, wafer, cell and module production. Its Shine range includes N-type TOPCon and Elan includes bifacial Mono PERC for utility and industrial work. Control of more manufacturing steps can support quality checks, but you must still inspect the delivered model and test report.

Selected Adani modules specify 35 mm anodised aluminium frames, 5,400 Pa front loading and 2,400 Pa rear loading. Those ratings matter for exposed coastal or elevated industrial roofs. A structural engineer must still check the roof and mounting design for local wind uplift.

Tata Power Solar

Tata Power Solar has operated for more than three decades and manufactures in Bengaluru and Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu. Its residential and commercial ranges may appeal when product tracking and an established claims process matter. Compare the offered module rather than treating the name as a substitute for specifications.

Tata offers may cost about ₹2 to ₹4 per Wp more than some competitors. That is an indicative comparison, not a fixed price. Ask what the premium buys in construction, inspection and warranty service. Tirunelveli is closer to southern Andhra Pradesh, which can simplify freight and shipment tracking.

Premier Energies

Premier Energies is headquartered in Hyderabad and has automated cell and module lines at E-City. It was an early South Indian producer of N-type TOPCon. For Tirupati, Srikalahasti or Chittoor, the shorter route from Hyderabad may help deliveries and technical follow-up.

Look for consistent cell appearance, sound packaging and matching flash data on the Premier model offered. Colour uniformity is useful for inspection but does not prove electrical performance. Careful handling matters even over a shorter freight journey.

Compare TOPCon with Mono PERC

P-type Mono PERC uses a passivated emitter and rear cell. N-type TOPCon uses a tunnel oxide passivated contact. The indicative figures below show why model selection can matter on a hot roof. Check each number against the current datasheet in your quotation.

What to compare P-type Mono PERC N-type TOPCon Effect on a rooftop
Silicon doping Boron-doped P-type Phosphorus-doped N-type Avoids boron-oxygen defects
Commercial module efficiency 20.8% to 21.4% 22.2% to 22.8% About 5% more capacity per sq ft with TOPCon
Power temperature coefficient -0.35% / °C -0.30% / °C Lower output loss in 42 degree C heat
First-year power loss 2.0% 1.0% Avoids boron-oxygen light-induced degradation
Annual power loss 0.55% / Year 0.40% / Year Higher cumulative output over 30 years at these rates
Bifacial response 70% ± 5% 80% ± 5% More response to reflected light behind the panel
Warranted output in year 30 ~84.8% ~87.4% 2.6 percentage points more warranted output

A Rayalaseema module can exceed 60 degree C on a summer afternoon. Above the standard test temperature of 25 degree C, power falls according to the specified coefficient. At 60 degree C, the example PERC rate of -0.35% per degree C gives a 12.25% reduction; TOPCon at -0.30% gives 10.5%. That gap may add roughly 2% to 4% on the hottest days, while annual output still depends on weather and layout.

Ray2Volt normally specifies Tier-1 N-type TOPCon bifacial modules for commercial work. We check the chosen model, shade pattern, mounting height and roof surface because bifacial gain and output per square foot depend on the installation.

Read the product and performance warranties

You receive two different promises with a panel. Keep the documents for both and check who handles a claim.

The 12-year product warranty

Ray2Volt quotes a 12-year panel product warranty. It concerns manufacturing defects such as delamination, junction box faults, diode damage, frame warping and moisture entering the encapsulant. The manufacturer's warranty sets out the remedy, so read the conditions for inspection, transport and replacement before you need them.

The 30-year performance warranty

The performance document sets a minimum output curve under specified testing conditions. For the example TOPCon curve used here, check these limits:

  • During year 1, the example curve allows no more than 1.0% loss. Ask how the maker accounts for light-induced degradation.
  • From year 2 to year 30, the example annual linear loss is capped at 0.40%.
  • At the end of year 30, the example warranted output is at least 87.4% of nameplate power.

For exclusions and claim steps, read our panel warranty guide. Our maintenance guide explains how to keep cleaning and inspection records.

How Ray2Volt checks a shipment

Discount stock can contain factory rejects, used utility modules or panels with unclear ALMM history. A contractor may still advertise them as Tier-1. Compare the delivered serial numbers with the agreed bill of materials before installation.

Our home and business procurement checks include:

  • Authorised sourcing: We buy from the manufacturer or an authorised national distributor and keep the supply record needed for claims.
  • RFID and serial matching: We compare the embedded RFID record, back label and printed serial number with the maker's shipment information.
  • Flash-test evidence: We request factory readings for each panel's peak power, open-circuit voltage (Voc) and short-circuit current (Isc), matched to its serial number.

Treat altered barcodes, missing RFID records or serial numbers that do not match the paperwork as reasons to stop installation. Ask the supplier for a written explanation.

Frequently asked questions

What does the BloombergNEF Tier-1 ranking measure?

It measures manufacturer bankability. A qualifying maker supplies its own modules to at least six projects above 1.5 MW, financed without recourse by six different commercial banks within two years. It does not certify the build quality of the model on your roof.

Why choose N-type TOPCon instead of Mono PERC?

Selected TOPCon models exceed 22.5% efficiency against about 21.2% for some PERC panels, and specify -0.30% rather than -0.35% per degree C for heat loss. Bifacial response can reach about 80% rather than 70%. Together these may mean 3% to 5% more annual units on a comparable hot roof; confirm by model and site.

How much extra can DCR panels cost?

Allow an indicative ₹9 to ₹12 per Wp, or ₹9,000 to ₹12,000 per kWp, over comparable non-DCR panels. DCR includes Indian cell production and module assembly. Check the current List-II rules, your eligibility and the actual supplier quotation.

Which specification matters in South Indian heat?

Look at the model's power temperature coefficient. Waaree Elite TOPCon and selected Premier N-type bifacial modules quote around -0.30% per degree C. When ambient air exceeds 42 degree C, the panel itself gets hotter. Ask for a site-specific generation estimate rather than choosing solely by brand.

How do you check a delivered ALMM panel?

Match the exact model with the current ALMM entry. Check the invoice, back label, embedded RFID record and serial number against the maker's flash-test sheet. Records can identify the factory, cell origin, lamination date and unique module number.

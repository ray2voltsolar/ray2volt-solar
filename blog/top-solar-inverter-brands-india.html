Top Solar Inverter Brands in India (2026): Deye, Sungrow, Growatt & Polycab

Your inverter has to handle heat, changing sunlight and grid voltage every day. Compare four common brands by the exact model, service route and backup features you need.

What to compare

  • Inverters need a clear service plan. An NREL and Clemson University inverter reliability study cites field data in which power converters accounted for 43% to 70% of service calls.
  • Deye's hybrid range supports batteries and generator input. Check battery voltage and the exact model before assuming those functions are included.
  • Sungrow's larger string models quote peak efficiency up to 98.8%, I-V scanning and anti-PID features. Verify each feature on the offered unit.
  • Growatt and Polycab provide on-grid options for homes and smaller businesses. Compare local service availability and warranty paperwork as closely as efficiency.

Panel wattage is easy to compare on two solar quotations. The inverter deserves equal attention because it converts and controls the power you use. Panels are largely passive glass and silicon assemblies expected to work for decades. An inverter contains power electronics that switch continually while sunlight is available, so heat, dust and grid conditions matter.

At sunrise, a string inverter starts when DC voltage reaches roughly 120 V to 180 V. During the day it follows the panels' maximum power point as clouds change, handles DC input that can reach 1,000 V, and produces grid-matched 230 V single-phase or 415 V three-phase AC. In parts of Andhra Pradesh, it must also work through ambient heat near 45 degree C. A trip stops generation until the fault clears.

This guide examines Deye, Sungrow, Growatt and Polycab, with Microtek and Solis as other names you may see in a bid. We compare the model features that matter on a real roof and the questions to ask about repair. For the basics, see how an inverter works and our system type comparison.

Why inverters need careful placement

Field records vary by equipment age, site and maintenance practice. The US Department of Energy review cited above reports that power converters caused 43% to 70% of service calls in the studies it examined. That range is not a forecast for your roof. Control heat, voltage stress and contamination in the design, then ask the installer how quickly a failed inverter can be diagnosed and repaired.

Heat affects power electronics

IGBT or SiC switching devices and smoothing capacitors generate heat as current passes. A sheltered plant room or outdoor wall may reach 40 to 45 degree C at noon. A commonly used reliability rule says each 10 degree C rise in operating temperature can roughly halve an electrolytic capacitor's life. Look for suitable heat sinks, fan design and enough ventilation around the cabinet.

Grid voltage and frequency vary

A steady 230 V supply is easier on an inverter than a rural feeder that can move from about 170 V to 285 V with spikes and lightning surges. Sustained overvoltage can damage metal oxide varistors and AC filters. Compare Type II surge protection on DC and AC sides and the model's permissible trip window, often about 180 V to 275 V AC. Confirm it against your measured feeder.

Dust and moisture can reach circuits

Paddy chaff, stone dust or cotton fly can build up around an industrial inverter. If moisture reaches dust on a high-voltage board, leakage current can cause faults. Check whether the offered unit has IP65 or IP66 protection and whether its electronics are separated from cooling airflow. A rating does not excuse poor cable glands or an exposed mounting position.

Mount a string inverter in shade with the clearance required by its manual. Direct afternoon sun can raise enclosure temperature beyond 65 degree C. Thermal derating at that point may cut output by 20% to 40% during strong sunlight.

Compare Deye, Sungrow, Growatt and Polycab

No brand wins every installation. Match the model to the connection type, outage pattern, roof layout and service arrangements.

Deye for hybrid systems

Deye offers multi-mode hybrid models from 3 kW single-phase to 50 kW three-phase. Some residential units work with 48 V or 51.2 V lithium iron phosphate batteries, while larger models use high-voltage packs. Confirm the battery range for the exact inverter: a 50 kW table entry must not be read as a 48 V model.

On suitable Deye models, an auxiliary port accepts a diesel generator or microgrid input. Dry-contact control can start a generator when the battery runs low, while controls prevent reverse feed. Some three-phase models allow 100% unbalanced output, useful when one phase carries more office or production load than the others. Ask for the wiring and permitted load schedule.

Sungrow for larger commercial roofs

Sungrow began in 1997 and reports more than 400 GW installed globally. Its commercial string ranges include SG33CX, SG50CX and SG110CX models for projects from roughly 33 kW through 125 kW and above. Compare a specific model with your transformer's capacity and string layout.

Selected Sungrow units quote European efficiency of 98.4% to 98.6%. The iSolarCloud I-V scan can help locate cell cracks, diode faults or shade across many modules, with some scans taking under 15 minutes. Anti-PID recovery on equipped models addresses potential induced degradation overnight. Verify the functions and warranty for the proposed model rather than assuming a 25-year inverter life.

Growatt for homes and SMEs

Growatt's MIN range serves single-phase homes, while MID and MAC models cover larger three-phase installations. Compact housings, OLED controls and permitted DC oversizing up to 150% can make them practical where wall space is tight. Have the designer check the oversizing limit for the precise unit and local temperature.

Some Growatt models start at around 140 V DC, which can help capture low-light hours if the string design supports it. Ask the installer to confirm startup voltage, warranty support and the local service route. A lower purchase price is useful only if a failed unit can be diagnosed promptly.

Polycab for local supply access

Polycab has an extensive Indian electrical distribution network, including dealers and service contacts across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Its PSIS on-grid range carries BIS certification for applicable models. Confirm the certificate and service contact for the model actually supplied.

Selected Polycab units quote peak efficiency around 98.2% to 98.4%, against higher figures for some Sungrow models. Regional stock may help with a board replacement in Srikalahasti or Tirupati; a cited 48 to 72-hour dispatch window should be confirmed in writing for your location and capacity. Do not base a purchase on an assumed repair promise.

Choose on-grid or hybrid by outage need

The main difference is what happens during a grid cut. Check the essential loads you need to keep running before paying for a battery-capable system.

On-grid inverters disconnect during outages

Indian grid-connected systems must meet CEA technical requirements for anti-islanding. If the utility supply fails, the inverter disconnects so solar cannot energise a line a DISCOM worker expects to be dead. The cited disconnection time is within 20 milliseconds for the relevant design. A pure on-grid plant therefore stops supplying power during a cut, even at noon.

Hybrid systems can form an isolated supply

A compatible hybrid inverter uses transfer switching and a bidirectional DC converter to separate the building from the grid. With a charged battery and suitable panel output, it can then supply selected lights, computers or refrigeration as a local microgrid. Some models quote a transfer below 10 milliseconds. Check the backed-up load list, battery size and actual transfer rating before calling it uninterrupted.

If grid cuts total less than 15 hours a month, an on-grid proposal may produce faster returns. Typical business payback is 3 to 5 years after a site audit. For daily load shedding or diesel use, compare a Deye hybrid quote with the generator's operating cost, which may exceed ₹26 per unit. All figures are indicative, schemes can change and you should confirm eligibility for your case. Ray2Volt does not give tax or investment advice.

Check service and claims before purchase

A high efficiency figure is of little use while a failed unit waits for a replacement board. Ask for the warranty contact, fault reporting process, regional stock position and expected return merchandise authorisation, or RMA, time.

  • Sungrow: Reported engineering offices include Bengaluru, Mumbai and New Delhi, with service staff in South India. The cited 5 to 9 business day commercial board replacement is an estimate; ask for the current service commitment.
  • Polycab: Its electrical network includes Vijayawada, Hyderabad and Chennai. The quoted 48 to 72-hour regional dispatch for standard units depends on stock, so confirm it for your model.
  • Deye: National distributors, including Loom Solar and specialist partners, may hold controller boards and power modules. The cited replacement period is 7 to 12 days; obtain the named local partner and current stock position.
  • Growatt: Support includes a technical hotline and authorised centres in Bengaluru and Mumbai. The ShinePhone app can send error logs for a remote diagnosis before a claim is processed.

Match inverter choice to system size

Ray2Volt uses its site audit and experience across Tirupati, Srikalahasti and Chittoor to shortlist models. These are starting choices, not fixed rules for every roof:

  • Homes, on-grid, 2 kW to 10 kW: Polycab or Growatt single-phase or three-phase models with two MPPTs can suit simple roofs. Confirm cooling noise, grid voltage tolerance and shade pattern.
  • Homes or businesses, hybrid, 3 kW to 20 kW: Deye with compatible lithium iron phosphate storage can serve essential loads and accept a suitable generator. Size the battery from the outage duration.
  • Factories, 30 kW to 200 kW+: Sungrow or Polycab three-phase string models offer multiple MPPTs, remote monitoring and DC oversizing options. Check the actual enclosure rating, protection design and roof strings.

Frequently asked questions

Which inverter service route should I check in South India?

Polycab's electrical distribution is broad across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Sungrow has commercial technical support in Bengaluru and Chennai, while Deye uses authorised distribution partners. Ask each bidder to name the regional service provider and confirm stock for your model.

What makes Deye useful for a hybrid project?

Across its hybrid range, Deye offers 48 V and high-voltage battery options, generator input, unbalanced three-phase output on selected units and transfer ratings below 10 milliseconds. Those features vary by model. Check battery compatibility and the critical-load wiring in the proposed design.

Why compare European efficiency with peak efficiency?

Peak efficiency is measured at a favourable voltage and load, often around 98.4% to 98.8% for these examples. European efficiency weights several operating points and may be around 97.5% to 98.2%. Use it to compare likely conversion under changing sunlight, then check the exact datasheet.

Can I add a battery to an on-grid inverter later?

A standard Sungrow SG or Polycab PSIS on-grid inverter does not take a DC battery directly. You can replace it with a compatible hybrid unit or add an AC-coupled battery inverter. The latter can add about 6% to 10% conversion loss, so plan future storage before finalising today's equipment.

What inverter warranty does Ray2Volt provide?

The inverter warranty on Ray2Volt systems is 8 to 10 years, depending on the model. Ask for the manufacturer document, start date, exclusions and local claim route when comparing two offers.

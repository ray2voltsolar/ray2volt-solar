Free power audit

What the audit covers

The audit is complimentary for prospective customers. It looks at your electricity usage, the system size that suits your site and your potential savings, and gives you:

  1. System capacity

    An indicative kWp range based on your bill and available roof.

  2. Project cost

    An indicative investment range before technical verification.

  3. Savings and payback

    Estimated bill reduction, annual savings and payback direction.

  4. Funding direction

    Whether buying outright (CAPEX), finance or RESCO suits your business.

Have these ready if you can

  • Recent electricity bills, ideally the last 12 months
  • Access to the roof or shed where panels could go
  • Someone who knows when the site uses the most power

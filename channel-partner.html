Know someone who wants solar? Introduce them.

If you're an electrician, contractor, builder or fabricator, your customers already ask you about solar. Introduce an interested customer to Ray2Volt Solar and our team does the rest. When the project meets the payment conditions, you receive a referral fee under a written scheme.

Channel partners

Who it's for

  • Electricians and electrical contractors
  • Electrical and general EPC companies
  • Civil contractors and builders
  • MEP contractors, fabricators and roofing contractors

What we need with a referral

  • The customer's name and working phone number
  • Their location
  • The latest electricity bill or EB service number, if you have it
  • Confirmation that the customer knows we will contact them
  • For a business: the business name and who makes the decision

The ground rules

To protect your customers and our name, partners don't quote Ray2Volt prices, promise subsidies, loans, savings or dates, collect money from customers, or present themselves as Ray2Volt staff. Referral fees are earned only under the written scheme.

There's no training, portal or meeting needed before your first referral.

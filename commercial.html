Why commercial solar

Power your business growth

Solar can reduce your electricity cost by up to 80% - savings that flow straight to your bottom line, based on your site assessment.

Whether you run a factory, warehouse, hotel, hospital, or office building, a well-designed solar plant can dramatically cut your energy bills while demonstrating your commitment to sustainability.

Besides rooftop plants, we build ground-mounted solar plants for businesses with open land. We also deliver open access solar, where power from a plant away from your site reaches you through the grid (how ground-mounted solar and open access work). For backup, we deliver solar + battery hybrids and solar + diesel-generator hybrids (compare solar + battery with diesel).

Instead of paying ever-increasing tariffs, you generate your own power at a fixed, predictable cost - with savings starting once your system is commissioned.

Ownership

Flexible ownership models

Choose the model that works best for your business.

CAPEX model

Own your solar system outright.

  • Strong long-term savings potential

  • Accelerated depreciation benefits

  • Full ownership of the asset

  • May qualify for applicable government incentives

Best for: Businesses with capital available for investment and seeking strong long-term ROI.

OPEX / PPA model

Zero upfront investment. Pay only for power consumed.

  • No capital investment required

  • Savings begin once your PPA rate is confirmed

  • No maintenance hassles - we handle it

  • Fixed power rate for contract duration

Best for: Businesses wanting to save without capital investment.

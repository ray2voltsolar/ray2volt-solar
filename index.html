Ways to pay

Buy it, borrow for it or pay per unit

Pay for the plant once (CAPEX), spread the cost with a loan, or pay only for the solar power you use (OPEX). Homes can add the PM Surya Ghar subsidy.

Compare finance options

Homes and businesses

Buy outright CAPEX

Pay for the plant once, own it and keep all of the bill savings. Businesses that pay income tax can claim 40% depreciation on the plant.

How depreciation works

Homes and businesses

Bank loan EMI

Own the plant and spread the cost over monthly instalments. We guide you through the loan, with collateral-free options where eligible.

Work out your EMI

Businesses

RESCO OPEX

A developer funds, installs and owns the plant, and you pay an agreed rate for the solar units you use. Offered site by site.

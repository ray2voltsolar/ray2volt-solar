Solar for Warehousing & Logistics

Your roof is your biggest unused asset. Put it to work.

Monetise Every Square Metre of Roof

Warehouses have enormous, unobstructed rooftops - ideal real estate for MW-scale solar plants.

Distribution centres and logistics parks combine vast metal roofs with growing power needs: lighting, conveyors, sorting automation, and increasingly, EV fleet charging. Rooftop solar serves all of it at a fraction of grid cost.

Even facilities with modest self-consumption can benefit - surplus generation can be net-metered or structured as an additional revenue stream, turning a passive roof into an income-producing asset.

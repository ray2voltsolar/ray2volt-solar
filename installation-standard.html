Our installation standard

Every part and every test we commit to on a rooftop plant, written down so you can hold us to it, and compare it with any other quote.

Draft for owner review. Not linked from the site and not indexed. Every highlighted item needs Ray2Volt's real value, and each value must be one the site team actually delivers on every job. Remove this box before publishing.

1. Mounting structure

The structure holds the panels for 25 years or more, through monsoon rain and strong winds. Its steel, finish and design decide whether it rusts, bends or lifts.

  • Material and section: To confirm: e.g. hot-dip galvanised steel, section sizes for sheds and RCC roofs
  • Galvanising thickness: To confirm: coating in microns and the standard it follows
  • Fasteners: To confirm: stainless steel or galvanised, grade
  • Height above the roof: To confirm: standard and elevated options

2. Wind design

  • Design wind speed: To confirm: the wind speed each structure is designed for, and the code used (for example IS 875 Part 3)
  • Who signs off the design: To confirm: in-house engineer or external structural engineer, and for which project sizes

3. Anchoring and waterproofing

  • RCC roofs: To confirm: chemical anchors, ballast or civil pedestals, and how the roof is sealed
  • Metal sheds: To confirm: clamp or purlin fixing method and how leaks are prevented

4. Cables

  • DC cable: To confirm: type (solar-rated), brand, size by string current
  • AC cable: To confirm: type, brand, sizing rule and allowed voltage drop
  • Routing and protection: To confirm: conduit or cable tray, UV protection

5. Earthing and lightning protection

  • Earthing pits: To confirm: number and type for DC, AC and lightning arrester
  • Target earth resistance: To confirm: value in ohms
  • How it is tested: To confirm: instrument and when the reading is recorded
  • Surge and safety devices: To confirm: DCDB and ACDB contents, SPD type

6. Panels and inverters

  • Panels: tier-1 brands, on the ALMM list; DCR panels where the project requires them. To confirm: the brands currently offered
  • Inverters: 8 to 10-year product warranty, depending on the inverter model. To confirm: the brands currently offered

7. Commissioning checklist

  • To confirm: the tests done before handover, e.g. insulation resistance, string open-circuit voltage and current, earth resistance, inverter settings, protection checks

8. The handover file

What you receive when the plant is handed over:

  • To confirm: e.g. single-line diagram, panel and inverter serial numbers, warranty cards, test results, net-metering documents, monitoring login, maintenance guide

The work behind the plan

Care for more than the panels.

The core maintenance scope below is included in each plan, with frequency matched to your selected schedule and equipment.

Cleaning & routine checks

At every scheduled visit

  • Solar panel cleaning using suitable methods
  • Inverter exterior, ventilation and accessible filter care as specified by the manufacturer
  • Visual panel, mounting, cable and equipment checks
  • Inverter status, operating readings and alarm checks
  • Visit report with photographs and findings

Preventive maintenance

Quarterly in every plan

  • Panel, clamp, mounting and corrosion inspection
  • Cable, connector, conduit and cable-tray inspection
  • AC/DC distribution-box checks for visible damage, moisture or overheating
  • Visual earthing, lightning protection and surge-protection indicator checks
  • Manufacturer-specified connection torque checks under safe isolation
  • Labels, shutdown instructions and maintenance-record checks

Electrical health & support

Annual tests, plus ongoing support

  • Earthing resistance measurement
  • Insulation resistance testing where applicable
  • Tests performed with appropriate isolation by qualified technicians
  • Phone / WhatsApp support during business hours
  • Remote troubleshooting and manufacturer-approved guidance
  • Generation reviews, summaries and warranty support at your plan level

Choosing your plan

A little clarity
before you decide.

What does “per kWp per year” mean?

The annual price is based on the total installed DC capacity of your solar modules. For example, a 100 kWp plant is ₹40,000 on Essential, ₹55,000 on Plus or ₹60,000 on Pro per year, before applicable GST and additional work.

Why do Plus and Pro have the same number of visits?

Both include monthly on-site maintenance. Pro adds weekly remote generation reviews, monthly performance summaries, priority business-hours support and manufacturer warranty claim coordination.

Is one cleaning every month always enough?

Cleaning needs vary with dust, birds, nearby activity and seasonal conditions. The plan includes cleaning at the scheduled visits. If your site needs extra cleaning, we can quote an additional schedule after assessment.

Can I book just a repair or diagnostic visit?

Yes. Tell us about the issue and your plant. One-time fault diagnosis, repairs and specialist tests are assessed and quoted separately from the annual plans.

Do these plans replace my existing warranty or free service?

No. Share your current agreement so we can check existing coverage and avoid overlapping services. RayCare does not extend manufacturer warranties or change their terms.

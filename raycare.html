Good to know

Your RayCare
questions, answered.

Need help with a specific fault? Send a service request.

Can you maintain a plant installed by another company?

Yes. RayCare welcomes existing installations by other EPCs, subject to a site assessment. Equipment condition, documentation, safe access and service availability help us confirm the scope.

What is the difference between Plus and Pro?

Both include 12 scheduled site visits each year. Plus includes monthly remote generation reviews and an annual summary. Pro adds weekly remote reviews, monthly performance summaries, priority business-hours support and manufacturer warranty claim coordination.

Are repairs and replacement parts included?

Scheduled maintenance is included as described in your plan. Unplanned site visits, corrective repair labour, replacement parts, extra cleaning and specialist testing are quoted separately. We agree additional work with you before proceeding.

What do I need to provide at the site?

Suitable water and power, safe access to the plant and any relevant equipment or service records. Remote reviews require a working monitoring system, connectivity and permission to access its data.

Does RayCare guarantee solar generation?

RayCare provides scheduled maintenance and reviews to identify issues. Generation depends on weather, system design, equipment condition, shading and grid availability; the plans do not include a generation guarantee. Remote reviews happen at the stated plan frequency and are not continuous monitoring.

I already have Ray2Volt service coverage. Do I need RayCare?

Share your existing installation agreement with us first. We will check what is already covered before recommending a separate RayCare plan or any additional service.

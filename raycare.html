Questions

Good to know

Do I need a paid plan?

No. Free RayCare covers faults, a yearly health check and warranty claims for 5 years. A plan adds regular cleaning, which is what keeps output up on a dusty roof.

What happens after the first 5 years?

The manufacturer warranties carry on: 12 years on panels, 30 years on panel performance, 10 years on lithium-ion batteries and 8 to 10 years on inverters. A RayCare plan keeps your cleaning and health checks going.

What should I keep ready for a claim?

Your invoice and warranty card, a photo of the inverter display, and if you can, monitoring app screenshots showing when output changed.

Can I clean the panels myself?

Yes, if you can reach them safely. Use plain water and a soft brush, and avoid washing hot panels. Our solar panel maintenance guide explains how.

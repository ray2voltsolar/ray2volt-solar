What is an on-grid system?

An on-grid solar system is directly connected to the electricity grid. It generates power during the day, and any excess energy is exported to the grid, earning you credits through net metering.

This is the most affordable type of solar system since it doesn't require expensive batteries. It's perfect for homes with reliable grid supply and minimal power cuts.

  • Lower upfront cost, with no battery required

  • Export excess power to the grid and earn credits

  • Eligible for PM Surya Ghar subsidy up to ₹78,000

  • Minimal maintenance with Tier-1 components

Note: Does not provide backup during power cuts as it requires grid connection to operate.

Why on-grid solar

Benefits of on-grid solar

Here's why on-grid is the most popular choice for homeowners.

Significant savings

Cut eligible grid energy charges by up to 90%. On-grid home systems have an estimated payback of 4 to 5 years after the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, and generate free power for 25+ years. The payback is an estimate for a system bought outright, at current APSPDCL tariffs and typical daytime use.

Net metering credits

Earn credits by exporting excess power to the grid. Offset your nighttime and cloudy day usage seamlessly through APSPDCL net metering.

30 year performance warranty

Our Tier-1 panels come with 30-year performance warranty and 8 to 10 year inverter warranty. Peace of mind for decades.

PM Surya Ghar - free electricity

Get up to ₹78,000 subsidy on your rooftop solar installation under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana.

PM Surya Ghar runs until 31 March 2027 (MNRE scheme guidelines, as of 23 Sep 2026).

The Government of India's flagship program makes solar more affordable than ever. As an empanelled vendor, Ray2Volt handles the complete subsidy application process for you.

Subsidy benefits:

  • 1-2 kW: ₹30,000 per kW subsidy

  • 2-3 kW: ₹18,000 per kW (additional capacity)

  • Maximum subsidy: ₹78,000 for 3 kW system

Solar systems

Choose your system type

We offer two main types of solar systems to match your needs and budget.

On-grid solar system

Connected to the electricity grid. Most economical option.

  • Lower upfront cost - no battery required

  • Export excess power to grid and earn credits

  • Eligible for PM Surya Ghar subsidy

  • Minimal maintenance

Note: Does not provide backup during power cuts as it requires grid connection to operate.

Hybrid solar system

Grid-connected with battery backup. Best of both worlds.

  • Power backup during outages

  • Store excess energy for night use

  • Maximum energy independence

  • Still eligible for net metering benefits

Ideal for: Areas with frequent power cuts or homes requiring uninterrupted power.

Why solar

Benefits of going solar

Here's why thousands of homeowners are making the switch to solar energy.

Significant savings

Cut eligible grid energy charges by up to 90%. On-grid home systems have an estimated payback of 4–5 years after the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. This is an estimate for a system bought outright, at current APSPDCL tariffs and typical daytime use; your own figure depends on your tariff, how much solar you use yourself and the system cost.

Power backup

With hybrid systems, enjoy uninterrupted power even during grid outages. Say goodbye to inverter hassles.

30 year performance warranty

Our Tier-1 panels come with a 12-year product warranty and a 30-year performance warranty. Peace of mind for decades.

Government scheme

PM Surya Ghar - free electricity

Get up to ₹78,000 subsidy on your rooftop solar installation under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana.

PM Surya Ghar runs until 31 March 2027 (MNRE scheme guidelines, as of 23 Sep 2026).

The Government of India's flagship program makes solar more affordable than ever. As an empanelled vendor, Ray2Volt handles the complete subsidy application process for you.

Subsidy benefits:

  • 1-2 kW: ₹30,000 per kW subsidy

  • 2-3 kW: ₹18,000 per kW (additional capacity)

  • Maximum subsidy: ₹78,000 for 3 kW system

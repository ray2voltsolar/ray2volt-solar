Bill review

What we read from your bill

  • Units used: your monthly consumption in kWh, and how it changes through the year.
  • Tariff category: whether you are billed as industrial, commercial or another category, and on LT or HT supply.
  • Contracted or sanctioned load: this limits how large a plant can be connected.
  • Charges: the energy charge per unit, fixed or demand charges, and time-of-day charges where they apply.

What you get back

  • An indicative kWp range based on your bill and available roof.
  • An indicative investment range before technical verification.
  • Estimated bill reduction, annual savings and payback direction.
  • Whether buying outright, a loan or RESCO suits your business.

A final quotation follows a technical site visit. Photos of your roof or shed help us too, if you can take them.
